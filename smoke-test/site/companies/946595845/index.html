Aanonsen Jernvarer AS · wholesale of china and glassware and cleaning materials · Tønsberg

Company brief

Aanonsen Jernvarer AS is a private limited company in Tønsberg, founded in 1988, working in wholesale of china and glassware and cleaning materials (industry code 46.440). [1]

On its own website (aanonsen.as) it describes itself as: "Aanonsen er en ledende distributør innen kjøkkenredskaper, bakeutstyr og produkter til hus og hjem …". [2]

The general manager (daglig leder) is Michael William Jones. [3]

Its 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 15.8 million (up NOK 1.5 million, +11% on 2024), an operating profit of NOK 960 thousand and a net profit of NOK 383 thousand (against a profit of NOK 159 thousand in 2024). [4]
