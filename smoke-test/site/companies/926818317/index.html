SPEDIFY AS · warehousing and storage · Tinn

Company brief

SPEDIFY AS is a private limited company in Tinn, founded in 2021, working in warehousing and storage (industry code 52.100). [1]

On its own website (spedify.no) it describes itself as: "Spedify leverer 3PL i Norge, fulfillment, lager for nettbutikk, retur, reparasjon og markedsplassklare operasjoner for vareeiere med høyere krav". [2]

The general manager (daglig leder) is Stian Mårdalen. [3]

Its 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 41.2 million (up NOK 30.0 million, +267% on 2024), an operating loss of NOK 7.5 million and a net loss of NOK 5.0 million (against a loss of NOK 5.2 million in 2024). [4]

Latest dated event: employee count updated to 77 (2026-09-14). [1]
