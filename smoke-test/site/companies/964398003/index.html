FOLKSOM AS

FOLKSOM AS · other real estate activities on a fee or contract basis · Tønsberg

Company brief

FOLKSOM AS is a private limited company in Tønsberg, founded in 1992, working in other real estate activities on a fee or contract basis (industry code 68.320). [1]

On its own website (folksom.no) it describes itself as: "FOLKSOM er en ledende utvikler og forvalter av næringseiendom, bolig og stedsutviklingsprosjekter i Vestfoldregionen". [2]

The general manager (daglig leder) is Pål Grabow Bakke Egeland. [3]

Its 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 5.2 million (down NOK 2.1 million, -28% on 2024), an operating loss of NOK 7.2 million and a net loss of NOK 5.6 million (against a loss of NOK 16.4 million in 2024). [4]

Latest dated event: auditor registration changed (2026-10-05). [3]
