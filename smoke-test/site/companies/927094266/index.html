SPORTLET.NO AS · retail sale of clothing · Herøy

Company brief

SPORTLET.NO AS is a private limited company in Herøy, founded in 2021, working in retail sale of clothing (industry code 47.710). [1]

On its own website (sportlet.no) it describes itself as: "Sportlet.no forsyner deg med kvalitetsklær og utstyr for Sport og Fritid. Her er det alltid 30-60% rabatt på kjente merkevarer". [2]

The general manager (daglig leder) is Eldar Lillerovde. [3]

Its 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 1.8 million (down NOK 644 thousand, -26% on 2024), an operating loss of NOK 283 thousand and a net loss of NOK 359 thousand (against a loss of NOK 155 thousand in 2024). [4]

Latest dated event: board registration changed (2026-07-24). [3]
