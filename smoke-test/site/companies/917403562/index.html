Company brief

TRÆNA SNEKKERSERVICE AS is a private limited company in Træna, founded in 2016, working in construction of residential and non-residential buildings (industry code 41.000). [1]

Marcin Pawlak is both general manager (daglig leder) and chair of the board. [2]

Its 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 2.1 million (down NOK 1.5 million, -42% on 2024), an operating profit of NOK 59 thousand and a net loss of NOK 61 thousand (against a profit of NOK 99 thousand in 2024). [3]
