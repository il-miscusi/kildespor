Company brief

AF GRUPPEN ASA is a public limited company in Oslo working in construction of residential and non-residential buildings; its 2025 group (consolidated) accounts show revenue of NOK 31.99 billion, up 4% on 2024, and a net profit of NOK 1.29 billion. [1][2]

It is hiring: 1 open job ad, the latest "AF Maskin - Platearbeider"; its latest dated news is "AF Gruppen bygger studentboliger og næringsarealer for Diakonhjemmet stiftelse" (2026-10-07) on its own website. [3][4][5]

Amund Tøftum is general manager and Morten Grongstad chairs the board. [6]
