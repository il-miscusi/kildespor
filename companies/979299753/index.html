Company brief

HJELPEMIDDELSPESIALISTEN AS is a private limited company in Oslo working in wholesale of pharmaceutical and medical goods, with 33 employees in the register; its 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 216.2 million, up 14% on 2024, and a net profit of NOK 22.4 million. [1][2]
