Company brief

SOLVANG ASA (Solvangship) is a public limited company in Stavanger working in freight ocean transport, with 50 employees in the register; its 2025 group (consolidated) accounts show revenue of USD 211.9 million, down 8% on 2024, and a net profit of USD 69.9 million. [1][2][3]

No open job ad was found on its careers page or in NAV's vacancy feed; its latest dated news is "Partnering up with Nemo Maritime" (2026-06-01) on its own website. [4]

Edvin Endresen is general manager and Michael Steensland-Brun chairs the board. [5]
