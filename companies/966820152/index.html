NORDIC DOOR AS · Manufacture of doors and windows of wood · Lyngdal

Company brief

NORDIC DOOR AS is a private limited company in Lyngdal working in manufacture of doors and windows of wood, with 181 employees in the register; its 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 415.8 million, up 13% on 2024, and a net profit of NOK 8.3 million. [1][2]

No open job ad was found on its careers page or in NAV's vacancy feed; its latest dated news is "Åpningstider sommeren 2026" (2026-06-12) on its own website. [3]

Øyvind Arnesen is both general manager and board chair; it is a subsidiary of L T L AS (935803802). [4][5]

Compared with the previous run, no published fact changed in the sources re-checked; on record, the net result moved from a loss of NOK 5.2 million in 2024 to a profit of NOK 8.3 million in 2025, and the latest register change is the employee-count update to 181 (2026-10-13). [1][2]
